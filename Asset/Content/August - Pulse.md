## THIS EDITION'S FOCUS

**In the UAE, who owns the invoice when it breaks?**

E-invoicing on the Peppol 5-corner model becomes mandatory in the UAE from 1 January 2027, in XML format only. The rollout is phased by business size. Businesses face an AED 5,000 monthly penalty until e-invoicing is enabled. There is also a AED 100 fine for each invoice that is not issued or transmitted.

Every accredited platform looks identical in a demo. The differences surface at integration, at cutover, and on the day the FTA changes the rules. So the real question is not what the platform can do. It is who owns the invoice when it breaks.
