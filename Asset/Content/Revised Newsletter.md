The e-Invoicing Brief

The latest compliance changes are already shaping finance and tax priorities across markets. Here are the updates worth keeping on your radar this month.

| France   France's e-invoicing mandate is now entering Phase 1\. From 1 September 2026, all businesses must be able to receive e-invoices, while large and mid-sized businesses must issue them. ClearTax is listed as an approved Plateforme Agréée (PA) on DGFiP's registry. If France is in your footprint, this is now live, not upcoming. Compliance needs to be part of day-to-day invoicing. | Slovakia   Slovakia's e-invoicing infrastructure is live — the Financial Administration's provider directory opened on 3 June 2026 and 59 certified delivery service providers are accredited, with over 5,000 Slovak businesses and organisations having already selected one voluntarily. The mandate for domestic B2B and B2G invoices takes effect 1 January 2027\. Provider selection should start now — waiting until Q4 2026 risks a bottleneck before the deadline. |
| ----- | ----- |
| **United Arab Emirates**   The UAE has moved to full accreditation for e-invoicing service providers. ClearTax is now a fully accredited provider with accreditation number 163162\. Businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more must appoint an accredited provider by 30 October 2026\.  If you're above the AED 50 million threshold, provider appointment is now a hard deadline, not a planning item. | **Malaysia**   Malaysia has raised its e-invoicing exemption threshold from RM1 million to RM3 million. Businesses below RM3 million may now be exempt, although certain businesses within larger groups remain in scope.  Don't assume the new threshold clears your whole group. Instead check entity-level exposure before relying on it. |
| **Brazil**   Brazil has published new technical specs linking e-invoices to payments under its split-payment framework. IBS and CBS validation checks begin 1 September 2026\. Review invoice and payment flows now to confirm the required tax data is captured before validation starts. | **South Africa**   SARS has opened consultation on a national e-invoicing model using a five-corner framework, with phased rollout from 2030\. Comments close 16 October 2026\. 2030 feels distant, but the consultation window is the moment to start tracking. Get South Africa on your radar for scenario planning. |
