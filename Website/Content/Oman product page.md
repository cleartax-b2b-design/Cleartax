**Subheadline:** Oman uses a Peppol-based 5-corner model, also called a decentralised continuous transaction control and exchange model. The OTA became an official Peppol Authority in January 2026\. ClearTax handles every requirement out of the box.

**Format** XML (UBL 2.1) · PDF/A-3 · PINT OM E-invoices must be in XML (UBL 2.1) or PDF/A-3 format, aligned with the Peppol International PINT Oman specification published by the OTA. The PINT OM standard defines Oman-specific data structures, validation rules, and exchange processes while maintaining interoperability with the global Peppol network.

**Archival** 10-year secure digital retention All e-invoices must be securely archived digitally for at least 10 years for audit and verification. ClearTax stores every invoice in structured, immutable format — no manual prep for audits ever.

**Validation** Multi-layer pre-transmission checks The ASP performs multi-layer validation including structural XSD, business rules, and code list compliance before any invoice reaches the Fawtara network. ClearTax catches errors before they become failures.
