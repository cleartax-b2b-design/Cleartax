**Philippines EIS Implementation Timeline**

**Feb 2025 — Issued** RR No. 011-2025 published. BIR establishes mandatory e-invoicing for large taxpayers, e-commerce businesses, and exporters.

**Sep 2025 — Extended** BIR issues RR No. 026-2025, extending Phase 1 deadline to 31 December 2026, recognising the complexity of system transition.

**31 Dec 2026 — Phase 1: Mandatory** Large Taxpayers (LTS), e-commerce businesses, and ERP users must issue structured e-invoices and transmit data to BIR's EIS in real time.

**2027 onwards — Phase 2: Expanding Scope** Registered Business Enterprises (RBEs) and additional taxpayer groups brought into scope. Timelines subject to future BIR Revenue Regulations.
