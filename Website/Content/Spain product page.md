## **FOLD 3: Key Requirements (Compliance Checklist)**

**Section heading:** What Spain Requires — and How ClearTax Delivers It

**Card 1 — Verifactu**&nbsp;  
Every invoice must carry a hash-chained integrity record with a QR code, transmitted to AEAT in near-real-time. Verifactu is a separate obligation from B2B exchange — ClearTax handles both with a single submission pipeline.

**Card 2 — True Copy Obligation (Copia Fiel)**&nbsp;  
A faithful UBL copy of every invoice must reach AEAT simultaneously with delivery to the buyer. This applies regardless of which delivery channel you use. ClearTax automates this dual-path transmission so nothing falls through the gap.

**Card 3 — Acceptable Formats**&nbsp;  
Spain supports UBL 2.1, Facturae (version to be confirmed), CII, and EDIFACT for buyer delivery. UBL is mandatory for the copia fiel to AEAT. ClearTax converts from your native ERP format — no restructuring of your data model required.

**Card 4 — Lifecycle Reporting**&nbsp;  
Buyers must report invoice acceptance, rejection, and payment status to AEAT within 4 calendar days (excluding weekends and national public holidays) of receipt. ClearTax tracks every invoice through its full lifecycle and surfaces exceptions before they become audit triggers.

**Card 5 — Interoperability**&nbsp;  
Private platforms must interoperate with each other and with the AEAT public solution. Automated retrieval from AEAT is a platform obligation, not a business one. ClearTax is built for it.

**Card 6 — Applicability**&nbsp;  
Applies to Spanish-established entities in domestic B2B transactions. Foreign entities are in scope only if they hold a Spanish permanent establishment. B2C and B2G (which remains under FACe) are excluded.

## **FAQs**

**What is Verifactu and does it apply to my business?** Verifactu is Spain's certified billing system framework under Royal Decree 1007/2023. It requires invoicing software to guarantee the integrity, traceability, and immutability of every invoice record — with QR codes and near-real-time AEAT transmission. All CIT taxpayers must comply from January 2027; autónomos and IRPF taxpayers from July 2027\.

**What formats are accepted for B2B e-invoicing in Spain?** UBL, Facturae, CII, and EDIFACT are all accepted syntaxes for invoice exchange. Peppol BIS is also valid on private platforms as it uses UBL/EN16931. All formats must conform to the EN16931 semantic data model.

**Does Spain require AEAT clearance before an invoice reaches the buyer?** No. Spain's model is post-audit, not clearance-based. Invoices are issued directly to buyers via accredited private platforms or the AEAT public solution. AEAT receives the copia fiel simultaneously but does not need to approve the invoice before it reaches the buyer.

**What happens if AEAT updates its technical specifications?** ClearTax's compliance engine updates automatically when AEAT publishes new requirements — including the ongoing evolution of the Crea y Crece public platform. You don't need to track BOE bulletins or run emergency development cycles.

**What are the penalties for non-compliance?** Non-certified billing software under Verifactu can trigger fines of up to €50,000 per fiscal year. Failures to issue or accept structured B2B invoices under Crea y Crece can result in penalties up to €10,000 per infraction. Invoice errors carry fines of 1–2% of transaction value, up to 75% in suspected fraud cases.
