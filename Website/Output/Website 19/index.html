02Monthly close

03VAT exposure

It can quote the rate. It cannot check who used it.

Exposure is the value of every transaction that applied the wrong rate. Finding it needs both:

All 40,000 line itemsRate history by date

It has neither.

Tests every line item against the rate in force that day.

Surfaces what broke and what it is worth — before you file.

And two million transactions never will. No link exists between a clause and the transactions it governs.

Every term maps to the transactions it governs.

Checked continuously, so leakage surfaces in the month it happens.

It does not know your supply chain logic:

Joins PO, shipment, receipt and invoice into one chain.

Where orders stall, what the delay costs, what sits in transit — daily.

06Tax notice response

No invoice numbers, no dates, no counterparties — just a generic provision. The officer asks for the same proof again.

Drafts from the actual transactions.
