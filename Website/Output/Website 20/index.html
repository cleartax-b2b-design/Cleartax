02 · The offer — agent catalogue

Agents for the CFO's office

You have already tried this with generic AI. The outcomes become real when the same models have your foundation.

For the CFO conversation 4 agents
01
CFO Dashboard Agent

Walk into the monthly review with insights, not numbers.

Builds the CFO presentation from your data, in the format and narrative the tax team controls.

02
Tax Governance Agent

An objective read on where you stand, not a self-assessment.

Scores six dimensions — reconciliation health, ITC quality, notice exposure, invoice rejection rates, Table-4 accuracy and vendor compliance — with independent commentary on each, benchmarked against industry peers.

03
Audit and Advisory Agent

The monthly CA audit, on every entity, without adding headcount.

Maps your GL, assesses completeness, calculates an independent position and compares it to what you filed. Maps findings to known notice patterns to score risk.

04
Notice Dashboard Agent

Notice tracking through your lens, not a generic dashboard.

Combines ClearTax notice data with your own tracking rules. You define your own controls.

For money stuck with the government 2 agents
05
Litigation Agent

Every notice type answered with the transactions behind it, inside the deadline.

Drafts from the actual records: invoice, line item, date, counterparty, GSTIN and the rule in force on that date. At-scale reconciliation, case law from verified databases.

06
Refund Agent

The bottleneck is data preparation, not filing.

Reads your specific scenario — inverted duty, excess payment or unutilised balance — and prepares a complete dossier: refund quantum, supporting data and justification trail.

For the monthly close 4 agents
07
180-Day Reversal and Reclaim Agent

Section 16(2)(b), computed per document.

Takes your Table-4 data and open payments report from SAP or your ERP, identifies documents needing reversal, eligible for reclaim and falling due next month, and calculates reversal and interest per document.

08
ERP Writeback Agent

Codify your transformation logic once, then it runs itself.

Learns the transformation once from your writebacks and a sample ingestion file. Your team approves the logic; from then on it applies automatically every month.

09
RCM and Ineligible Classification Agent

A prioritised review queue instead of a line-by-line scan.

Auto-triages every line item using ledger codes, vendor master data and transaction commentary, assigning High or Medium confidence for RCM applicability and ITC ineligibility.

10
Advance Adjustment Agent

Catches unmatched advances before they become a penalty.

Tracks advances and matches them against invoices received, flags gaps before they become compliance issues, and generates the adjustment recommendations.

05 · The operating contract

Three rules, across every agent

The same operating contract everywhere, so your team learns it once and trusts it everywhere.

01 Human in the loop Every agent surfaces a decision and waits for approval before acting. Your team stays in control.
02 Data in, action out Agents take your raw data — ERP exports, GL or GSTN data — and return the artefact you need: an Excel sheet, a dossier, a dashboard. Approved outputs execute on the platform automatically.
03 Repeatable, not one-shot For monthly workflows, the agent learns your logic once, then runs every month with full backworking on every number.
